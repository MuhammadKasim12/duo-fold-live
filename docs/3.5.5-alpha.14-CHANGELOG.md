
## Wallpaper studio and visual refresh

- Choose Custom Wallpaper now opens an integrated Duo screen with independent cover and inner photos.
- Drag/pinch to frame each photo, or use zoom and position sliders. Reset framing, use one photo on both screens, save explicitly, or discard edits.
- An atomic album manifest publishes both choices. Legacy single-photo selections remain the fallback; existing saved photos are not automatically replaced or enabled.
- Native wallpaper, retained windows, and Home/wake layers share the same panel selection and crop math. Physical display identity is retained across D0/D1 remaps; fold/power/animation timing is unchanged.
- Existing wallpaper controls remain under Advanced Settings. Its original photo picker deliberately replaces the per-screen album with one legacy photo; lock-screen actions keep their original behavior.
- A shared dark palette, typography, rounded cards, responsive margins, illustrated wallpaper entry, and refreshed hero bring the main app, setup, and studio together.
- Adds the open-source hobby-project disclaimer to the app footer (already published on GitHub).

This is a preview build. Validate different photos and crops through folding, rotation, sleep/unlock, disable/re-enable, and process restart on the phone. The previously investigated remap flash remains paused and is not claimed fixed here.
