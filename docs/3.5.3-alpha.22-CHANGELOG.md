# 3.5.3-alpha.22 — Incoming app endpoint hold experiment

Developer setting **Incoming app screenshot hold (experimental)** defaults ON, only for dual-screen screenshot/window-first mode. Existing power and task-return behavior is unchanged.

Captures the exact routed app on D1 near the endpoint, before the closing app-first return. Capture requires a drawn exact-task window, unlocked screen, unchanged physical identity and dimensions, and excludes animation layers. Secure content is not requested. If the app moves before capture completes, skip the hold.

Two noninteractive overlay hosts follow the incoming physical panel across logical D1/D0 remapping. The hold releases after two exact-app HAS_DRAWN observations on D0 at destination dimensions plus a 64 ms settle. There is a hard 1.8-second deadline from capture request, including capture time. Stale data, lock/sleep, direction reversal, new session, disabled mode, reader stop, and service destruction clear it. Late callbacks cannot reinstate it. Slow folds may exhaust the deadline before the endpoint; fast folds may outrun capture, in which case the existing behavior continues.

Diagnostics record capture/skip, host visibility, frame commit, app readiness and release reason. Commits and HAS_DRAWN do not establish optical visibility or a fresh post-remap app buffer. This experiment targets the wallpaper/Home flash after power returns; it cannot display during physical panel OFF.

Test three open/close cycles in the Duo app with five seconds at endpoints. Keep other switches unchanged and Samsung ON hold OFF. Send the short report and describe blackout, wallpaper/Home flash, and whether the held screenshot transitions to live content. Toggle the new option OFF for a baseline if needed.
