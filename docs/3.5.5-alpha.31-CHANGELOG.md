# Duo Fold Live 3.5.5-alpha.31 — black cover reveal experiment

Pre-release. Alpha.30 visibly showed a brief frozen blur; its prepared layer committed before the cover's first reported ON state in both recorded closing handoffs. At the owner's request, replace that outgoing blur with solid black and a longer reveal.

- Reuse alpha.30's independent cover-sized compositor layer, pre-switch commit gate, physical-panel tracking, capture exclusion, and cleanup.
- Prepare an opaque black buffer once per geometry/session. Preparation sends only frame identity/timestamp/rotation metadata; no outgoing picture or blur is transferred for this layer.
- Stay black until a fresh cover animation frame commits, then fade the black layer away over 300 ms with smoothstep easing. The live animation continues underneath; this does not restart or freeze its hinge-driven progress.
- If readiness never arrives, wait at most 900 ms and perform the same 300 ms fallback reveal. Late readiness cannot restart the fade. Closure, reversal, lock, disabled state, missing mapping, and session loss still clean up promptly.
- Opening preview, handoff angles, glass settings, and 120 ms smoothing are unchanged. Software commit timings remain distinct from optical visibility.

Validation: curve/shader checks; unit tests for hold, reveal, missing/late readiness, monotonic opacity, and destination identity/geometry; CI lint, release assembly and signature verification.

Device test: same settings, slow/normal inner-to-cover folding on Home and a fullscreen app. Report whether the clear flash is hidden and whether the black-to-animation reveal feels too long or too short. Include the status report. This remains experimental until visually confirmed.
