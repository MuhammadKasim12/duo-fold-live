# 3.5.8-alpha.6 — Prepare both live glass renderers

Restore closing prewarm after the alpha.5 bypass provided no observed speed improvement.

Normal live glass now retains separate cover and inner SurfaceViews and shader instances. Each buffer keeps its panel dimensions across primary remaps. The standby renderer receives bounded hidden warm-up draws from outgoing content at most once per 180 ms; it does not report destination readiness or drive motion-return state. Both surfaces are excluded from capture. Selection clears previous readiness generations and waits for a new active buffer post before revealing the selected view.

Geometry is discovered from exposed display modes. On a first run, a panel not yet exposed cannot be allocated at its real size until Android exposes it. Offscreen warm-up is not proof of visible pixels, and destination app content still needs a fresh validated capture. The retained window itself still follows D0; this experiment removes child-renderer resizing, not all WindowManager relayout.

No blur algorithm, motion sensitivity, 60 ms return, reveal duration, handoff threshold, screenshot path or reflected preview changes. No claim of speed improvement before phone testing. Stable and Play unchanged.

Test both directions in the same fullscreen app after a complete fold cycle. Include status and an Observer light capture. Look for PREPARED_RENDERER_SELECT and PREPARED_RENDERER_POST, and compare fresh GLASS_COMMIT timing. Watch for wallpaper flashes, crops, doubled effects or orientation regressions. A preparation buffer never qualifies as a fresh destination frame.
