# 3.5.8-alpha.15 — neutral, broader blur falloff

Neutral background is now the default. On first launch after upgrading, turn off Samsung background adjustment and the alpha.14 solid-mask diagnostic once. Later user selections persist; rendering-mode selection is retained.

Replace the constant-opacity core with a continuous quintic opacity falloff sampled into 65 disjoint strips. A squared-distance profile keeps blur visible farther across the sweep and smoothly fades at its edge. Coverage at half progress increases from about 68% to 84% of the eligible region. Inner coverage remains limited to the original half in every rotation. Radius calculation, projection, handoff and rotation behavior are unchanged. This is an opacity feather approximation, not a native variable-radius diffusion kernel.

Keep the A/B controls and retained blur diagnostics. Device visual validation is still required. CI runs unit tests, lint, rendering regression checks, release audit and signature verification.
