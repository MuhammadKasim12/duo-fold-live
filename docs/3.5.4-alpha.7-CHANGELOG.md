# 3.5.4-alpha.7 — Closing release timing experiment

- Delay Duo's normal cover-state release by a fixed 1000 ms at the closed endpoint. A separate timer releases the owned request even if angle polling stops; repeated closed samples cannot renew it.
- Abort the hold on stale/invalid input, lock/sleep, route disable, or teardown. Reopening resumes normal handoff. Android may cancel the request independently; this is logged and the old timer is discarded.
- Add CLOSE_DELAY ARM, TIMER, END, and ABORT markers beside explicit release and Android cancellation events. Timestamps and observed scheduling delay are included.
- Suppress the closing direct-power pulse in this experiment, even if its saved toggle is ON. Keep that toggle OFF for comparison. No power policy writes are added.
- Keep the prepared fold-only recovery available for up to 2000 ms after closure to cover the delayed release (still bounded by its existing closing lease).
- Preserve alpha.5 geometry and alpha.6 prepared fold-only recovery. This is a timing experiment, not a claimed blackout fix. The inner panel may remain on for the extra second if Android permits the hold.
- Test: normal mode, fully close and keep closed for at least 3 seconds. Repeat slowly and quickly, then copy diagnostics. Note whether the blackout moves approximately one second later. Also verify reopening during the delay and power-button sleep.
