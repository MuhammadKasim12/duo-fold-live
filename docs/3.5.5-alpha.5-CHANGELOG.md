# Duo Fold Live 3.5.5-alpha.5

Prerelease: custom wallpaper wake preparation defaults and toggle recovery.

- Wake preparation is enabled by default, including upgrades from earlier opt-in test builds. The old test preference is replaced; a deliberate override in this build is remembered.
- The preparation toggle and report button are at the bottom of Developer settings. Hold the toggle for three seconds to disable; tap to re-enable.
- Re-enabling rebuilds owned Home photo attachments and clears retry state. Successful Home attachments no longer consume the retry allowance permanently. Reattachment failures are included in the wake report.
- User reports no flash with alpha.4 continuously enabled; no claim yet that toggle recovery is optically fixed.

## Device checks

1. Upgrade, leave preparation enabled, and repeat Home → lock → wake → unlock. Stay on Home for three seconds on at least one cycle to exercise the completed handoff.
2. A short tap must not disable preparation; a three-second hold must. Release early or scroll away to cancel the hold.
3. Disable, lock/unlock once, tap to re-enable, then repeat the first test. Copy the wake report if any flash returns.

Stable 3.5.4 and other animation, folding and root settings remain unchanged.
