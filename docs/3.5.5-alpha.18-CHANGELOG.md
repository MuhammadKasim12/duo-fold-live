# 3.5.5-alpha.18

- Removes the app-level Support notifications toggle and ignores its legacy saved opt-out value. Support notification delivery is enabled for new and existing installs, subject to Android permission/channel settings.
- Moves the Notifications card to the bottom of expanded Advanced Settings. Running notification settings remain available.
- Preserves reminder scheduling, snooze, stop/donation retirement, and preview behavior.
- No display handoff, fade, wallpaper, or renderer changes.
