# alpha.41 — adjustable moving Plain Fade wipe

Replaces the fixed spatial shading mask with a translating soft wipe edge. Default travel spans 0–100% of the cover width or inner edge-to-hinge region, tracking normalized hinge progress linearly. The alpha.39 square-root dimming remains the default underneath; the pre-handoff black inner hold remains unchanged.

Plain Fade-only controls, separately for cover and inner: start/end position, motion start/end timing, timing exponent, softness, wipe opacity, start/end brightness and dimming exponent. Preferences persist and are applied on slider release. Brightness adjusts the animation overlay, not system brightness. Copy settings and per-panel reset actions are included; settings also appear in the diagnostic report. Other styles do not use these values.

Validate travel and direction on both panels, setting persistence, no cover previews, and black hold before handoff. Send copied settings to establish final defaults. Prerelease only.
