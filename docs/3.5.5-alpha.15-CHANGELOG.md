
## Screenshot handoff at the endpoint

- Removes the mid-fold primary-display promotion from window-first Screenshot Mode. The outgoing physical panel remains primary while the incoming fullscreen app stays live on D1.
- Opening releases concurrency at the configured open angle. Closing uses Android’s close release, with the existing bounded 1000 ms fallback.
- Both directions wait for the incoming physical panel to be primary, ON, and destination-sized before returning the exact app to D0.
- The incoming screen smoothly fades to black over the last 12 degrees before the configured closed/open angle. At default settings this is 14° → 2° closing and 160° → 172° opening.
- Independent, non-touchable accessibility overlays track the incoming physical panel through the D1/D0 remap. They are excluded from live captures. Reversing before the endpoint fades the mask back down.
- After the endpoint, the mask reveals once the exact app is ready twice, with a short settling period. A non-renewable 1800 ms deadline bounds the hold; connection loss, lock/sleep, disabling the mode, or reopening clears it.
- The old mid-fold Samsung ON-override experiment is inactive on this route. Status reports describe the endpoint fade and record its start, handoff, readiness, and reveal.

Normal Live Screen mode, wallpaper studio, and wallpaper rendering are unchanged. Software checks do not prove optical continuity: this build intentionally accepts Samsung’s native blackout at the endpoint and tries to blend it with the fade. Fast gestures can outrun sensor/display updates; validate both directions and reversals on the phone.
