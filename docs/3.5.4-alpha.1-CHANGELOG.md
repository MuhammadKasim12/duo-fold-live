# 3.5.4-alpha.1 — Native-resolution custom wallpapers

- Remove the 1600/1920-pixel photo import limits in settings, setup and the experimental wallpaper path.
- Keep enough source detail for native cover (1248 × 1972) and inner (2448 × 1848) rendering, including rotation. Larger detected built-in panels automatically raise the import size.
- Preserve existing centered framing, without stretching the photo. Small originals are not artificially upscaled during import.
- Save imported JPEGs at quality 100. Remove the experimental wallpaper renderer's additional 2048-pixel downsampling.
- Re-select the original photo after updating: previously discarded detail cannot be recovered from the saved image.

Prerelease for device testing; the latest stable remains 3.5.3. Animation and wallpaper lifecycle behavior are unchanged.
